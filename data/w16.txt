제목: What Time Is It?
(몇 시예요?)
"What time is it?"
"It's six o'clock. It's ____ to get up!"
"몇 시예요?"
"6시야. 일어날 시간이란다!"
"What time is it?"
"It's seven o'clock. It's time to wash."
"몇 시예요?"
"7시야. 씻을 시간이지."
"What time is it?"
"It's seven thirty. It's time to eat breakfast!"
"몇 시예요?"
"7시 30분이야. 아침 먹을 시간이란다!"
"What time is it?"
"It's eight o'clock. It's time to go to school."
"몇 시예요?"
"8시야. 학교 갈 시간이구나."
"What time is it?"
"It's eleven o'clock. It's time to play!"
"몇 시인가요?"
"11시예요. 놀 시간이지요!"
"What time is it?"
"It's three o'clock. It's time to clean up."
"몇 시예요?"
"3시란다. 청소할 시간이야."
"What time is it?"
"It's nine o'clock. It's time to go to sleep."
"Good night!
"Good night!"
"몇 시예요?"
"9시야. 잠잘 시간이란다."
"안녕히 주무세요!"
"잘 자렴! "
"What time is it?"
"It's ten o'clock. It's time to get up."
"Oh no!" cry the other animals.
"몇 시예요?"
"10시란다. 일어날 시간이구나."
"이를 어째!" 다른 동물들이 소리치네요.
