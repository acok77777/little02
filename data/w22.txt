제목: Amusement Park Fun
(놀이공원의 즐거움)
Going to an amusement park is fun.
놀이공원에 가는 것은 재미있어요.
There are many things to do.
할 것이 많아요.
There are many things to ride.
탈 것도 많고요.
There are many things to play.
놀 것이 많지요.
There are many things to eat.
먹을 것이 많고요.
There are many things to buy.
살 것도 많지요.
There are many things to watch.
구경할 것도 많답니다.
I like the amusement park.
나는 놀이공원을 좋아해요.
It is a lot of fun!
그건 아주 재미있거든요!
